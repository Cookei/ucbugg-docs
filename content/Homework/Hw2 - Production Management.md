# Individual Work

## Assignment

Complete the following [[Production Management]] lab

### Deliverables

> [!todo] Todo: Gradescope Questionnaire
> Complete the questionaire on Gradescope with the required relevant screenshots. There should be 3 total screenshots necessary.
> 
> 1. Your `UCBUGG_fall_2026` folder, containing your `homework` and group’s project folders.
> 2. Your group’s Google Drive folder open on your web browser (Google Chrome, Safari, Firefox, etc.)
> 3. Your <samp>Current Project</samp> dropdown in Autodesk Maya, where you set the project. It should show both the `homework` and group’s directories in it.

# Group Work

**Project Managers:** distribute the following work among members of your group, and make sure each item is stored in your group's shared folder so all group members can access it.

## Deliverables

> [!todo] Todo: Character Sheets
> Draw your character from the front, back, and sides Make sure your characters are in T-pose (if they stand on two legs) and have proportion lines (horizontal lines to maintain the same size and shape).

> [!todo] Todo: Asset List
> Fill out the Asset List, which lists all the things you will need for your short (i.e.: characters, props, set models, etc.). Try to be as thorough as possible.
>
> Your **Project Manager** should have already created the Project Management Spreadsheet which contains the Asset List inside of your group folder.

> [!todo] Todo: Animatic
> Make your storyboards into a video and adjust timing to your liking. Then add sound effects and music.

> [!todo] Todo: [[Mood Board]]
> Compile a collage of images from either the internet or your own drawings to get a visual "board" that easily identifies and describe the "mood" of various things in your short (buildings, art style, colors, etc.)
>
> You can read up more on what a mood board entails [[Mood Board|here]].
> You can use Google Slides, [Pinterest](https://www.pinterest.com/), [Google Drawings](https://docs.google.com/drawings/), [Milanote](https://milanote.com/), [Canva](https://www.canva.com/), [Figma](https://www.figma.com/), etc.
