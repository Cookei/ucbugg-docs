# Assignment

> [!todo] TODO: Install Google Drive for Desktop
> Download [Google Drive for Desktop](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/13022292?hl=en). We will be using this as our main way of sharing files within groups for UCBUGG.

> [!todo] TODO: Create Storyboard
> Turn your very own story idea into storyboards! It doesn’t matter how strong the story is, try to come up with something fun! **There’s a chance your story will be selected to be worked on for the rest of the semester.**
> 
> - Develop your story: exposition → rising action → climax → falling action → resolution
> - Create storyboards:
> 	- 15-20 storyboards!
> 	- Focus on the action, do not finalize your character / environment designs yet. However, indicate the background when necessary
> 	- Rectangular format, one panel per slide
> 	- Feel free to use these [storyboard templates](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/downloads/storyboard-template/#video-storyboard-templates)!
> 	- Submit as a .pptx or .pdf (download it from Google Slides if you choose to use Google Slides)
> 	
> 	- **Restrictions**:
> 	   - MAX 3 characters
> 	   - No humans
> 	   - No 2D/Live Action or stop-motion animation
> 	   - Original characters only

> [!todo] TODO: Pitching Your Story
> You will have the opportunity to pitch your story to the rest of the class. The class will vote on the stories and the most voted get to be made into 3D animated shorts! 
> 
> **IMPORTANT: Pitching is entirely optional, but if you do not pitch, your story cannot be included as a voting option.**
> 
> Practice your pitch:
> - Must be 30 seconds!
> - Practice makes perfect! Be confident, speak clearly, and consider emoting your story. Get excited!
> - Don't cram too much in verbally, let your boards speak for themselves
> - Introduce yourself in the introductions channel on Discord!
> 
> > [!attention]
> > Your pitch should be 30 seconds MAXIMUM! Facilitators will be timing your pitches next week during class to ensure this!
> 
> If you would like a critique and submit your pitch and storyboards early and on time, post your work in the help channel on Discord.
